::::::::::::::::::::: prereq
Before joining *HPC Workflow Management with Snakemake*, participants should be able to

- login to an HPC system and [navigate a UNIX shell][shell-novice].
- edit text files with an editor available on the HPC system.
- [submit jobs to a job scheduler][hpc-intro] present on the HPC system.

It will also be helpful if participants have a basic understanding of [writing rules for build automation with Make][make-novice]
